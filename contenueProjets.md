## 001 — CIRCA

**Slug** : `circa`  
**Année** : 2024  
**Client line** : Branding system / music management (concept)

### Catégories
- Branding / Visual Identity  
- 3D Design  
- Motion Design  
- Type Design  
- Poster System  

### Tagline
A modular identity built to move — from print systems to looping 3D marks.

### Overview
CIRCA is a compact branding system designed for digital-first releases. The identity mixes strict grid rules, barcode-like details and a bold logotype, then pushes it further with 3D motion loops for hero sections and social cutdowns.

### Context
The goal was to create a recognisable mark that can scale from small metadata labels to large posters, while staying flexible enough for multiple artists and releases under one umbrella.

### Approach
- Define a symbol + logotype pair with strong silhouette readability  
- Build a grid/label language (indexing, timestamps, technical notes)  
- Design a poster system that stays consistent across formats  
- Translate the identity into short, seamless motion loops  

### Deliverables
- Logomark & logotype  
- Poster series + outdoor mockups  
- Layout rules & grid  
- 3D logo exploration  
- Looping motion snippets for web/social  

### Tools
- Illustrator  
- InDesign  
- Blender (or Cinema4D)  
- After Effects  

### Notes
Concept case study — visuals created for portfolio exploration.

## 002 — TECHUNTER / Polymer Workshop

**Slug** : `techunter`  
**Année** : 2025  
**Client line** : Exhibition + editorial system (workshop)

### Catégories
- Art Direction  
- Editorial Design  
- Spatial / Exhibition Design  
- Photography  
- Branding / Visual Identity  

### Tagline
A research-driven exhibition language inspired by performance culture and product storytelling.

### Overview
TECHUNTER is a workshop project that turns “product research” into a visual system. Wall panels, modular plinths and printed layouts share the same brutal typography, restrained palette and data-like annotations.

### Context
The challenge was to keep a clean museum-like presentation while adding a “lab / parsing data” layer — giving every element an indexed, documented feel without losing readability.

### Approach
- Create a strict typographic hierarchy for titles, captions and metadata  
- Design modular panels that can scale across brands and topics  
- Use close-up photography to elevate material and details  
- Keep layouts sparse: white space + sharp alignment rules  

### Deliverables
- Visual identity + typographic system  
- Exhibition wall panels  
- Print/editorial layouts  
- Signage / plinth branding  
- Photo direction for product details  

### Tools

Workshop / exploration project.

**Année** : 2024  
**Client line** : Spec campaign / editorial portrait study

### Catégories
- Art Direction  
- Photography  
- Campaign / Concept  

### Tagline
Outdoor gear as armor — minimal studio portraits and product macros.

### Overview
A monochrome studio series exploring silhouette, protection and detail. The focus is on sculptural outerwear, futuristic eyewear and rugged footwear — treated like technical objects rather than fashion items.

### Context
The intention was to build a strong visual rhythm: wide negative space, hard lighting, and tight crops to highlight seams, textures and functional design cues.

### Approach
- Alternate between portrait frames and macro product details  
- Use strict grayscale to unify materials and shapes  
- Emphasize protective styling and technical silhouettes  
- Keep compositions centered, calm, and graphic  

### Deliverables
- Art direction for the series  
- Studio portrait set  
- Product detail shots  
- Simple campaign sequencing (hero → details → full looks)

**Client line** : Spec winter campaign study

### Tagline
Night riding, headlamps and motion blur — a cold, cinematic winter mood.

### Overview
NOCTURNE is a visual study built around contrast: wide alpine landscapes versus tight brand-led details. Light beams cut through snow, while long exposures add speed and unpredictability.

## 005 — Oakley — Visibility

**Slug** : `oakley`  
**Année** : 2024  
**Client line** : Spec campaign / night optics study

### Catégories
- Art Direction  
- Photography  
- Campaign / Concept  

### Tagline
Warm light vs. cold night — a visibility-driven optics mood study.

### Overview
A dark, cinematic series where snow particles and headlamp beams become graphic elements. The visuals highlight goggles and protection gear through silhouettes, reflections and high-contrast lighting.

### Context
The challenge was to make “visibility” feel tactile: particles in the air, harsh cones of light, and tight close-ups that suggest durability and performance.

### Approach
- Use single-source lighting to create strong beams  
- Lean into particle/grain for texture  
- Balance wide atmosphere frames with close-up product moments  
- Keep compositions minimal and high contrast  

### Deliverables
- Mood campaign photo set  
- Hero frames for web

- Sequence for a scrolling story
